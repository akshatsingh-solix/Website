SOLIX TECHNOLOGIES — BRAND GUIDELINES (SUMMARY)

Logo
- Use the primary logo on dark backgrounds (solix-logo-dark.svg) and the light variant on white/light backgrounds.
- Minimum clear space: height of the brand mark on all sides. Do not stretch, recolor, rotate or add effects.
- The brand mark (three stacked bars: Ember, Slate, Electric Teal) may be used alone at 24px or larger.

Colors
- Midnight #020817 (primary background)
- Ember #F97316 (primary action / highlight)
- Electric Teal #00D4FF (secondary accent)
- Slate #94A3B8 (secondary text)

Typography
- Headlines: Outfit (Medium / SemiBold)
- Body: IBM Plex Sans (Regular / Medium)
- Labels & data: JetBrains Mono

Name usage
- "Solix" or "Solix Technologies" — never "SOLIX Tech" or "Solix Inc" in running copy.
- Product names: Solix Enterprise Edition, Solix Common Data Platform (CDP), Solix Enterprise Archiving, Solix Application Retirement, Solix Enterprise Data Lake, Solix eDiscovery, Solix Consumer Data Privacy, Solix Enterprise AI, SOLIXCloud.
